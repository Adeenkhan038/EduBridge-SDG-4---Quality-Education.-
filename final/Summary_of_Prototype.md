EduBridge Prototype — What It Can Do
------------------------------------

EduBridge is a **mobile/Web, lightweight peer tutoring platform** with three user roles, each with their own set of capabilities.

🎓 As a Student (Rural High Schooler)
-------------------------------------

*   **Create an account** and set up a profile with their school, grade level, and STEM subjects they need help with
    
*   **Browse available tutors** and filter them by subject (Physics, Add Maths, Chemistry, etc.)
    
*   **View a tutor's full profile** — their university, subjects offered, a short bio, availability, and star rating
    
*   **Book a 1-on-1 tutoring session** by selecting an available time slot and confirming the request
    
*   **View upcoming and past sessions** from their personal dashboard
    
*   **Rate a completed session** with a star rating and short comment to give feedback
    

👨‍🏫 As a Tutor (University Volunteer)
---------------------------------------

*   **Create a tutor profile** with their university, year of study, subjects they can teach, and a bio
    
*   **Set weekly availability** (e.g., Monday 7–9pm, Thursday 6–8pm) so students know when they can be booked
    
*   **View incoming session requests** from students
    
*   **Accept or decline** a booking request
    
*   **View their upcoming and completed sessions** from their dashboard
    
*   **See feedback and ratings** left by students after sessions
    

👔 As a Coordinator (School Admin)
----------------------------------

*   **Log in to a coordinator dashboard** with an overview of all tutoring activity
    
*   **Review and approve new tutor applications** before tutors go live on the platform
    
*   **Monitor active tutoring pairs** — which students are being helped and by whom
    
*   **See sessions broken down by subject** to check curriculum alignment
    
*   **Track session completion rates** and overall program health
    

⚙️ Platform-Wide Capabilities
-----------------------------

*   **User registration and login** for all three roles
    
*   **Role-based access** — students, tutors, and coordinators each see a different dashboard
    
*   **Session confirmation screen** shown after a booking is made
    
*   **Mobile-first design** — works cleanly on basic Android smartphones with limited data
    
*   **Simple, low-data interface** — no heavy images or video required to use the core features
