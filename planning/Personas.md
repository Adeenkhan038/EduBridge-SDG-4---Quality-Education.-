Persona 1 – Rural STEM Student
Name: Aina Binti Rahman  
Age: 16  
Location: Rural secondary school in Kelantan  
Role: Form 4 student (Science stream)

Background:
- Lives in a small town; nearest tuition centre is more than 30 km away.
- Parents are supportive but have limited income; can’t afford regular private tuition.
- Shares a mid‑range Android phone with her younger brother; home internet is a prepaid data plan.

Goals:
- Wants to improve in Physics and Additional Mathematics to qualify for engineering courses after SPM.
- Hopes to find a mentor who can explain exam‑style questions in simple language.
- Wants more confidence asking questions without feeling embarrassed in a big classroom.

Technology & Access:
- Comfortable with WhatsApp, TikTok and YouTube on mobile.
- Internet is sometimes slow in the evenings; usually studies after Maghrib.
- Prefers apps that load quickly and don’t use much data.

Pain Points:
- School teacher has to rush the syllabus; little time for 1‑to‑1 explanation.
- Existing online videos are useful but not personalised to her exact questions.
- Hard to find affordable, trustworthy tutors who understand the national syllabus.

How EduBridge Helps:
- Can quickly browse volunteer tutors by subject and topic.
- Can book short 1‑to‑1 sessions focused on specific SPM questions.
- Feels safer using a platform endorsed by her school, instead of random social media contacts.
