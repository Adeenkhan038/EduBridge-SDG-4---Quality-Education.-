Team Roles & Responsibilities – EduBridge (SDG 4 – Quality Education)
Our team is organised to cover all key Software Engineering and Agile activities required in the project brief, ensuring that planning, requirements, design, prototyping, testing and reflection are all clearly demonstrated

## 1. Product Owner & Scrum Lead – [ADEEN AFZAL KHAN]
 Owns and safeguards the *Product Vision* for EduBridge, ensuring that all work supports the goal of connecting rural high‑school students with university tutors under SDG 4 – Quality Education.
 - Maintains the Product Backlog: creates, refines and prioritises user stories for students, tutors and coordinators.
 - Sets up and manages the Trello board structure (lists, sprints, labels, members) and leads Sprint Planning, Review and Retrospective discussions.
 - Oversees the GitHub repository structure, making sure that planning, design, prototype, testing and final artefacts are stored in the correct folders with clear commit messages.
 - Monitors deadlines, and makes final decisions when there are conflicts about scope or priorities.
 - Leads requirements engineering for EduBridge: gathers, refines and documents user needs from the perspectives of rural students, university tutors and school coordinators


## 2. UX Analyst – [EEJEEN]
Writes clear user stories and acceptance criteria that feed both the Product Backlog and Trello cards, and keeps them updated when requirements change.  
- Designs and maintains the Use Case Diagram, use‑case descriptions and simple user‑flow diagrams that explain how key scenarios such as “find a tutor” or “book a session” work.  
- Defines personas and stakeholder profiles to ensure the solution focuses on real user problems and constraints (e.g., low bandwidth, limited devices).  
- Works closely with the Prototype & UI Designer to ensure that wireframes and screen flows accurately reflect the documented requirements.

3. Prototype & UI Designer – [TEE QIN YI]
Translates requirements and user stories into low‑fidelity wireframes and then into high‑fidelity prototype screens for the EduBridge interface (student views, tutor views and coordinator views).  
- Builds the main clickable prototype in a design tool (such as Figma/Canva/Slides), focusing on clear navigation, accessibility and mobile‑friendly layouts for rural students.  
- Ensures visual consistency and branding across all screens (colours, typography, button styles, spacing) and documents basic UI guidelines for the team.  
- Exports prototype screenshots and stores them in the GitHub design and prototype folders, linking them from relevant Trello cards.  
- Supports the team in preparing the live demo for the final presentation, choosing a smooth scenario that shows the complete end‑to‑end tutoring flow.


  ## 4. Testing & Documentation Lead – [KHUN THAW ZIN HTUN]
  Designs the Test Plan for EduBridge, including functional tasks (e.g., “student finds a tutor and books a slot”) and basic usability checks for the prototype.  
- Creates and maintains test cases and simple result logs, and coordinates short user‑testing sessions with classmates or target users to collect feedback on the prototype.  
- Summarises evaluation findings and improvements, documenting which usability issues were discovered and what design changes were made in response.  
- Leads the writing of the Testing/Evaluation, Agile Process/Trello Usage, and Reflections & Lessons Learned sections in the final report, inserting evidence from GitHub and Trello where relevant.  
- Reviews the overall professionalism and completeness of the report and slides (clarity, structure, visuals, citations) to ensure the submission meets academic and presentation standards.

  ## 5. Shared Responsibilities

  All team members contribute ideas to improve EduBridge and participate in Sprint Planning, Reviews and Retrospectives.  
 Everyone updates Trello cards as they start, review and complete tasks, and makes meaningful GitHub commits with descriptive messages that reflect their work.  
 Each member prepares to present their own contribution and understanding of SDLC and Agile practices during the final presentation.
