#EduBridge Test Plan


##1.Introduciton

This document is mainly used for targeting for testing stratergies on EduBridge prototype.
The objective is to ensure that the process runs smoothly and correctly.
Additionally, it is also to ensure that the function is easy to use for students and tutors.

#2.Testing Objectives

- Verify that the process of the students logging in and verification runs successful.
- Ensure that students can find or book with their desired tutors.
- Confirm that tutors are able to accept the students request.
- Identify usability issues in navigation and layout.

#3.Scope of testing

Features to be tested:
- Log in/Register
- Tutor search
- Booking system
- Notification system

#4.Testing participants

- Students
- Tutor
